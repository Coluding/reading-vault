① Physics faithfulness in video / world models

11 items

The dominant theme: can generative video models actually obey physics, and how do we measure it? Splits into benchmarks (do they fail, and by how much) and methods (how to fix it), converging on the vault's own PhyWorld/PhyGround line.

paperhighPhyGenBench

Meng et al., ICML 2025 — The seminal physics-commonsense text-to-video benchmark: 160 prompts / 27 physical laws / 4 domains with a hierarchical PhyGenEval judge. Best model (Gen-3) scores only 0.51; scaling and prompt-engineering are both shown insufficient.

arxiv.org/2410.05363 · papers/meng-2024-phygenbench
paperhighPhyWorldBench

Gu et al., ICLR 2026 oral — A 1,050-prompt benchmark adding an anti-physics tier (asking for impossible events) plus an MLLM-judge (CAP). Best model (Pika 2.0) reaches only 0.262 joint success. This is the benchmark PhyGround critiques for its closed-source judge.

arxiv.org/2507.13428 · papers/gu-2025-phyworldbench
papermedJudgeFit

Cao et al. — Physics VLM-judges disagree with humans and each other. A per-VLM discovered taxonomy ("each judge its own yardstick") lifts human agreement by ~32% across 16 VLMs — a critique of one-size-fits-all judging.

arxiv.org/2606.22918 · papers/cao-2026-judgefit
papermedCRONOS

Begiristain et al. — Intervention-based counterfactual-consistency benchmark: hold the physical event fixed, vary viewpoint/scene/appearance. Finding: video generators are alarmingly viewpoint-sensitive — evidence they pattern-match rather than model 3D physics.

arxiv.org/2605.23699 · papers/begiristain-2026-cronos
paperhighThe Invisible Hand of Physics

Esmati et al. — Asks whether video diffusion models internally encode physical structure even when their outputs violate physics. Argues the knowledge is present in the latents but not surfaced in generation — a representation-vs-generation gap.

arxiv.org/2606.05328 · papers/esmati-2026-invisible-hand-physics
paperhighPhyWorld

Zhao et al. (EmbodyX / Northeastern) — A physics-faithful world model for video generation combining flow matching with RL-based physics alignment. One of the vault's two anchor papers for this cluster.

arxiv.org/2605.19242 · papers/zhao-2026-phyworld
paperhighPhyGround

Lin et al. — Benchmarks physical reasoning (not just realism) in generative world models, and critiques prior benchmarks' closed-source judges. The method-side sibling to PhyWorld.

arxiv.org/2605.10806 · papers/lin-2026-phyground
papermedPhysAlign

Xiong et al. — A LoRA adapter aligning Wan2.2 image-to-video features to a V-JEPA2 teacher plus 3D depth for physics coherence, with no visual-quality tradeoff and free at inference. Method sibling to PhyWorld.

arxiv.org/2603.13770 · papers/xiong-2026-physalign
paperhighInference-time Physics Alignment

Yuan et al., FAIR / Meta Superintelligence Labs — Much of the physics-implausibility in SOTA video generators can be corrected at inference time by steering with latent world models — no retraining needed.

arxiv.org/2601.10553 · papers/yuan-2026-physics-alignment
papermedACWM-Phys

Xue et al. — Benchmarks action-conditioned video world models across rigid / deformable / particle / kinematics regimes. Out-of-distribution failure tracks task complexity, not physics category — and bridges the cluster to robotics.

arxiv.org/2605.08567 · papers/xue-2026-acwm-phys
paperhighDelta Tokens

Kerssies et al. — "A frame is worth one token": efficient generative world modeling by encoding each frame as a compact delta token relative to the previous frame, slashing the token budget for video world models.

arxiv.org/2604.04913 · papers/kerssies-2026-delta-tokens

② World models, policies & robotics

10 items

World models as simulators for control, action-conditioned policies, and the physical-AI stack — including three orienting blog essays and one org profile.

paperhighWorld Action Models are Zero-shot Policies

Ye et al. (NVIDIA et al.) — Introduces DreamZero, a 14B World Action Model built on a pretrained image-to-video diffusion backbone (Wan2.1-I2V-14B) that acts as a zero-shot robot policy — generation and action from one model.

arxiv.org/2602.15922 · papers/ye-2026-world-action-models
papermedWorld4RL

Jiang et al. — A two-stage framework using a diffusion world model as a frozen high-fidelity simulator in which to refine an imitation-learned policy via RL — decoupling policy improvement from real-robot rollouts.

arxiv.org/2509.19080 · papers/jiang-2025-world4rl
paperhighFlowWM

Porcher et al. — A stochastic visual world model that runs flow matching directly in the feature space of a frozen pretrained encoder rather than pixel space — cheaper, and stochastic-by-design for uncertain futures.

arxiv.org/2606.29059 · papers/porcher-2026-flowwm
paperhighDexMimicGen

Jiang et al. (NVIDIA) — An automated data-generation system turning a handful of human teleop demos into a large imitation-learning dataset for bimanual dexterous manipulation — attacking the data bottleneck.

arxiv.org/2410.24185 · papers/jiang-2024-dexmimicgen
papermedGPC

Shi et al. (Xue Bin Peng) — Generative Pretrained Controllers borrow the LLM recipe — large-scale generative pretraining — to build general-purpose, transferable motor-control policies for physics-based character animation.

arxiv.org/2606.29148 · papers/shi-2026-gpc-motor-control
paperhighTemporal Difference in Vision (TDV)

Daithankar, Gladstone, LeCun, Ji — A self-supervised recipe for learning image representations from video that drops strong inductive assumptions, using temporal differences between frames as the learning signal.

arxiv.org/2606.15956 · papers/daithankar-2026-temporal-difference-vision
bloghighModern AI Robotics from First Principles

Interlatent — A first-principles tour of physical-AI robot policies, framing a policy as a function from observations to actions and building up the modern stack (imitation, world models, RL) from there.

interlatent.com · blogs/interlatent-ai-robotics

soniajoseph.ai · blogs/joseph-world-models-interpretability

③ Reasoning, recurrence & architectures

6 items

Latent reasoning, looped/recurrent transformers, and rethinking how model capacity is allocated.

paperhighLatent Thought Flow (LTF)

Zou et al. — Makes LLM reasoning happen inside the model's continuous hidden space instead of decoded chain-of-thought tokens — more efficient latent reasoning without emitting intermediate text.

arxiv.org/2606.16222 · papers/zou-2026-latent-thought-flow
paperhighFixed-Point Reasoners (FPRM)

Movahedi et al. (Orvieto lab) — A non-hierarchical Looped Transformer for latent reasoning that makes deep looping trainable by switching to a fixed-point formulation — stable and adaptive depth as an inference-time knob.

arxiv.org/2606.18206 · papers/movahedi-2026-fixed-point-reasoners
paperhighPretraining Recurrent Networks without Recurrence

Kumar & Isola (MIT) — Introduces Supervised Memory Training (SMT), a way to train nonlinear RNNs without backpropagation-through-time — sidestepping BPTT's cost and instability.

arxiv.org/2606.06479 · papers/kumar-2026-supervised-memory-training
papermedTapered Language Models

Bayat, Behrouz, Courville — Transformers give every layer the same MLP width; this paper argues for tapering that width across depth, reallocating parameters for better compute-efficiency at fixed budget.

arxiv.org/2606.23670 · papers/bayat-2026-tapered-language-models
papermedA Compositional Framework for Open-ended Intelligence

Momennejad & Raileanu — A theoretical/position paper proposing a formal object for open-ended intelligence built from a finite set of compositional primitives. No experiments — a framework contribution.

arxiv.org/2606.15386 · papers/momennejad-2026-compositional-open-ended
paperhighRevisiting the Platonic Representation Hypothesis

arxiv.org/2602.14486 · papers/groger-2026-aristotelian-view
