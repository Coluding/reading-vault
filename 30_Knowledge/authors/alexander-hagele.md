# Alexander Hägele

PhD student, MLO (Martin Jaggi's lab) @ EPFL. Works on optimization and training dynamics for large-scale neural network training. Site: haeggee.github.io.

- [[blogs/haegele-magnitude-direction]] — "Improving Neural Network Training by Decoupling the Magnitude and Direction of Weight Vectors": Magnitude-Direction Decoupling (MD) — constrain weight direction to a sphere with a separately learned magnitude/gain to remove magnitude-direction interference, giving automatic LR transfer across width/depth, warmup-free training, and gains in MoEs. With Atli Kosson, Alejandro Hernández-Cano, Martin Jaggi (2026).
