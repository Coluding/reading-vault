# Sander Dieleman

Research scientist working on generative models (diffusion, flow matching,
audio/image generation); writes long technical explainers at sander.ai.

## Blogs

- [[blogs/dieleman-diffusion-integral]] — "Learning the integral of a diffusion model": survey + unifying tutorial on **flow maps** (networks that predict any point on a noise→data path from any other), organized via the three consistency rules (compositionality, Lagrangian, Eulerian) from Boffi et al.; maps the 2024–26 few-step-sampling literature (consistency models, MeanFlow, Shortcut models, Align Your Flow, …) onto that grid.
