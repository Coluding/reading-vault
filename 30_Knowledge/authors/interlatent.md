# Interlatent

Robotics company / blog whose stated mission is to "bridge the curious public with robotics and make it easy for anyone to deploy a robot." Publishes accessible, first-principles explainers of the modern physical-AI stack.

- [[blogs/interlatent-ai-robotics]] — "An Overview of Modern AI Robotics from First Principles": mechanism-first tour of VLA policies (VLM backbone + action expert), action chunking, flow-matching action generation, edge/cloud latency, the data bottleneck (simulation + egocentric video), the training ladder, and self-improvement (RECAP / π*₀.₆).
