# Elon Litman

Works on the optimal-transport view of attention.

- [[papers/litman-2026-attention-priors]] (2026) — Lead author. *You Need Better Attention Priors*: recasts attention as entropic optimal transport with an implicit uniform prior, and shows positional encodings are heuristic approximations of an EOT-derived prior term; introduces a principled learnable prior (incl. a dedicated key-only attention-sink bias).
