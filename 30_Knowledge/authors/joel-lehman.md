# Joel Lehman

- [[papers/lehman-2011-novelty-search]] (2011) — first author with Kenneth O. Stanley; foundational novelty-search paper in Evolutionary Computation. Demonstrates that selecting for behavioural novelty alone can outperform objective-based search on deceptive problems.
