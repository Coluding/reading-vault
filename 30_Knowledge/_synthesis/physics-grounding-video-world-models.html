Physics Grounding in Video & World Models

Vault synthesis · 12 papers · 2026-07-22 · source of truth: _synthesis/physics-grounding-video-world-models.md

Video generators are pitched as world simulators, but every measurement instrument agrees they render physics-violating scenes while looking photorealistic — "visual conflation" (Chen & Zhu). Three lines of work: benchmarks quantify the failure, interventions partially fix it, and probing shows the models internally encode physics they fail to render. The frontier question: where is the knowledge lost — and can the judges be trusted?

Formulation "Knowing physics" means different things: outcome adherence (PhyGenBench, PhyGround), counterfactual instruction-following (PhyWorldBench), invariance to nuisances (CRONOS), action-conditioned fidelity (ACWM-Phys) — progressively stricter tests. Memorized trajectories pass the first and fail CRONOS's viewpoint intervention: exactly what happens.
Judging is contested PhyWorldBench accepts closed MLLM judges (75.1 PC ROC-AUC); PhyGround rejects them and fine-tunes an open judge; JudgeFit caps fitted VLM judges at ρ≈0.48; WMReward finds VLM rewards near chance while latent-WM surprise works. Convergent: VLMs are weak physics perceivers. Twist (Esmati): the generator's own DiT states out-probe V-JEPA2.
Where the failure lives Training data (PhyGenBench, PhysAlign) vs output distribution (PhyWorld) vs sampling (Yuan). Esmati supplies the mechanism: physics is encoded internally but not expressed. All three fixes buy real but bounded gains (+0.10 Likert / +8.5 / +6.8 PhysicsIQ) — consistent with surfacing latent knowledge, not adding it.
Disagreement on scale CRONOS: Cosmos 2B→14B hurts physical success (22→14%). ACWM-Phys: DiT-S→M improves InD and OoD. PhyGenBench: scaling helps statics, fails dynamics. Not directly comparable — but the vault holds contradictory evidence that scale buys physics.

Synthesis

[analyst's view] The three lines triangulate one thesis: the representation is ahead of the generation. DiTs demonstrably build linearly decodable physical structure (Esmati; Joseph's emergence zone), yet outputs fail benchmarks because nothing in the objective or sampler makes that structure load-bearing. All three interventions couple a physics-sensitive signal back into generation — and two of three route through V-JEPA2 even though the generator's own internals carry a stronger signal: self-guidance from DiT states is the obvious unexplored combination. Meanwhile the measurement layer is shaky — JudgeFit's ρ≤0.48 ceiling and Gemini's 16.6% bias mean cross-benchmark numbers reflect judge choice as much as capability; PhyGround's human-anchored protocol and PhysicsIQ are the most trustworthy yardsticks. The settling experiment: run a CRONOS-style intervention test on an aligned model — if DPO/WMReward gains survive viewpoint changes, physics was genuinely surfaced; if not, alignment is another appearance shortcut.
